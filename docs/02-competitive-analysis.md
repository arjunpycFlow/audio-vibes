# Competitive analysis

Reviewed 2026-10-25. "Observed" = seen on the competitor's site. "Takeaway" = our recommendation (general reasoning, not from the source).

## 1. PCE — Pacific Coast Entertainment (gopce.com)

**Observed**
- Large, full-service live event company with offices in Huntington Beach (OC/LA) and San Diego.
- Nav is service-first: Production, Rental, Retail, Integration, Backline. Plus About, Careers, location pages (OC/LA, San Diego), Videos, Blog.
- Homepage: one short headline, a "Tell us about your project" form (name, email, phone, message), a client-logo strip (big brands, universities, churches), a section per service with a "Get Started" / "Rent Now" button, a long "Our work" case-study grid, a featured long-form testimonial, and star snippets from Google, Facebook and Yelp.
- Rentals: catalog in 10 categories (Audio, Backline, Lighting, Networking, Power, Rigging, Staging, Video, …), no public prices, a cart that goes to a checkout/quote page. Policies stated: pickup or delivery (fees apply), return by drop-off or driver pickup.
- Phone number repeated in header, sections and footer; business hours in footer; separate location pages.
- Built on Webflow.

**Takeaways for Audio Vibes**
- Copy: service-first navigation; a project form on the homepage; "Our work" with real event names; review snippets with source logos; dedicated location pages.
- Don't copy: breadth. We are not a full AV/staging/rigging house. Trying to look like PCE makes a small team look thin. Our angle is the opposite: *focused, personal, right-sized for small and mid venues.*
- PCE is the "big" option in our area. Positioning against it: responsive owners you talk to directly, sized and priced for 30–300 guest events.

## 2. Orlans AVL (orlansaudio.com)

**Observed**
- Smaller LA business (Los Angeles 90039, address given on request) — the closest analogue to Audio Vibes.
- Nav: Home, Rental Equipment (Sound, DJ Gear & Backline, Lighting, Video, Other Event Supplies), Rental Packages, Contact.
- Main CTA: "Call/text or email now for your custom quote". Offers warehouse pickup/return, delivery & setup, or full service.
- **Rental Packages page** groups by use case — Party, Meeting, Band, Concert — each with 4 tiers and **fixed public prices** (e.g. Party from $275 to $1,200; Concert from $1,195 to $3,975), listing the gear in each.
- À la carte gear lists: item name with brand/model/spec and a price (e.g. QSC CP8 speaker $50), no photos per item, no cart.
- Trust: Yelp 5-star badge, brand logos (Chauvet etc.), Instagram.
- Footer shows accepted payments: cards (Stripe link), Zelle, PayPal, Cash App, Venmo.
- Built on Wix. Homepage is light on copy and on proof (no event stories, no testimonials on the page).

**Takeaways for Audio Vibes**
- Copy: **use-case packages with visible "starting at" prices** — this is the strongest idea on either site for a small operator. It answers "what do I need and roughly what does it cost?" without a phone call.
- Copy: the three service levels (pickup / delivery & setup / full service) — explain them in one simple table.
- Copy: "call or text" — texting matters for this customer.
- Improve on: add photos, add "good for up to N guests / indoor vs outdoor" to each package, add real event stories and reviews, faster mobile pages, clear policies (deposit, damage, cancellation, weather).

## Summary: where Audio Vibes can win

| | PCE | Orlans | Audio Vibes target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size of events | Small → huge | Small → mid | Small → mid |
| Breadth | Full AV + staging + retail | Sound, DJ, some light/video | Sound first, a few add-ons |
| Prices visible | No | Yes (packages + items) | Yes, "starting at" on packages |
| Booking | Cart → quote | Call/text/email | Quote list → form, call, text |
| Proof | Strong (logos, cases, reviews) | Weak (Yelp badge) | Real photos + reviews from day one |
| Mobile speed | Average | Weak (Wix) | Excellent (static Astro) |
