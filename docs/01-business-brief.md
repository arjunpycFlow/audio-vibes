## Who the customer is (working hypotheses — validate)

"Small and mid-size venues" suggests:

- Venues without an in-house sound system or tech: restaurants, bars, breweries, wineries, event halls, community centers.
- Event organizers renting those venues: weddings and receptions, birthday/quinceañera/sangeet-type celebrations, corporate meetings and mixers, school and church events, cultural and community festivals.
- Performers who need a PA: DJs, small bands, speakers.

Each segment searches differently ("PA rental near me" vs "wedding sound Orange County" vs "speaker rental for party LA"). The site should have a clear path for the 2–3 segments that the owners actually want most.
