# Open questions for the owners

Ask these in the Kickoff session (see 07-playbooks.md). **Critical** ones block launch.

## Business (critical)
1. Which events do you most want more of? Rank: weddings, private parties, corporate/meetings, churches/schools, community/cultural festivals, bars/restaurants/venues (recurring), DJs/bands.
2. Typical event size you handle today, and the biggest you can handle.
3. Where are you based, and how far will you travel? Is there a place customers can pick up gear (address shown publicly or not)?
4. Rentals: do you offer pickup, delivery & setup, full service — all three?
5. Business email and which phone numbers go on the site (and on Google). Who answers texts?
6. Hours / response time you can promise (e.g. "reply within 2 hours, 9am–8pm, 7 days").

## Gear & pricing (critical)
7. List of gear you own (brand, model, quantity). Photos?
8. Are you OK showing "starting at" prices for packages? Current prices for common jobs.
9. Delivery fee approach (flat by area? per mile?). Deposit amount. Damage/loss policy. Cancellation policy. Payment methods.

## Proof (critical)
10. Photos/videos from past events we can use (with permission).
11. Past customers who would leave a Google review or a quote for the site.

## Brand
12. Is there a vector logo? Keep black + coral?
13. Any languages besides English the site should support?

## Later
14. Do you have insurance (COI requests are common from venues)?
15. Will you add lighting/uplights, projectors, karaoke, DJ services?
16. Who will update the site content after launch, and how comfortable are they with GitHub?
